# WETU Studio AI — Cinematic Realism QA Policy

Status: CORE PRODUCT QUALITY GATE.

## Quality dimensions

- identity: face, body, hair, eyes and signature features remain coherent;
- wardrobe: clothing remains consistent unless an explicit change is recorded;
- anatomy: face, hands, limbs and body geometry are checked for visible anomalies;
- materials: fabric, skin, hair, glass, metal and surfaces retain plausible texture and interaction;
- lighting: shadows, reflections, exposure and depth cues remain coherent within a shot;
- camera: framing, motion and perspective remain consistent with the storyboard;
- environment: houses, streets, landmarks and spatial relationships remain coherent;
- motion: physical movement and contact should not introduce obvious discontinuities;
- audio: speaker, language, timing and dialogue remain aligned;
- subtitles: text matches the authoritative source dialogue and timing;
- provenance: provider/model/reference information is retained where available.

## Gate levels

INFO = recorded observation.
WARNING = review recommended.
BLOCKER = project cannot be marked READY until resolved or explicitly overridden by an authorized user with an audit record.

## Principle

WETU does not promise perfect photorealism. It measures and exposes quality failures so the creator can regenerate, replace, edit or accept them deliberately.
