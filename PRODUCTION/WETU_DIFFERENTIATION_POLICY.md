# WETU Studio AI — Product Differentiation Policy

Status: CORE PRODUCT STRATEGY.

WETU is not defined as another prompt-to-video generator. Its differentiator is a persistent production system that protects creative intent from idea to final export.

## WETU signatures

1. **Character DNA** — a persistent identity object covering face, body, hair, eyes, signature features, wardrobe and references. The same character ID is reused across scenes, episodes and projects.
2. **World DNA** — persistent cities, houses, locations, architecture, environment and landmarks. A location is an entity, not a repeated prompt.
3. **Scene Memory** — each shot inherits the relevant character, wardrobe, location, dialogue and visual context automatically.
4. **Continuity Guard** — visual identity, wardrobe, location and timing changes are explicit. Unexpected changes are surfaced instead of silently accepted.
5. **Multilingual Story Intelligence** — source dialogue remains authoritative; translations, dubbing and subtitles remain linked to the original speaker and timing.
6. **Provider-neutral Generation Router** — WETU can select or switch generation providers without destroying project structure, references or history.
7. **Generation Provenance** — every generation can retain prompt, references, provider/model metadata, timestamp, request fingerprint and output hash.
8. **Human Creative Control** — WETU proposes and orchestrates; irreversible publishing remains user-controlled.
9. **Production Memory** — projects preserve generations, rejected variants, decisions, references and continuity context so creators do not restart from zero.
10. **African/global creative foundation** — Lingala and Swahili are first-class supported dialogue languages alongside French, English, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic; cities, architecture and cultural contexts are modeled as reusable production entities rather than generic scenery.
11. **Low-bandwidth workflow** — metadata, references, scripts and project state can remain lightweight even when media generation is expensive or remote.
12. **One project, many formats** — the same story assets can feed advertising, social clips, short films, films, series and episodes.

## Design principle

**WETU remembers the production, not just the prompt.**

Competitor products increasingly combine generation, editing, references and multiple models in one workspace. WETU therefore differentiates through persistent structured creative memory, continuity enforcement, multilingual/cultural support, provider independence and auditable production history rather than claiming that a single model will always generate the best image or video.

## Non-negotiable

No marketing claim may describe a feature as superior merely because it is present. Differentiation must be demonstrated through reproducible product behavior and tests.
