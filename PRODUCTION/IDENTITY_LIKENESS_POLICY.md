# WETU STUDIO AI — Identity & Likeness Policy

Status: CORE PRODUCT / SAFETY CONTROL

Characters must remain visually identifiable and consistent across scenes. Photorealism is a product target, not a guarantee that every generated frame will be pixel-identical.

## Identity classes
- FICTIONAL: original photorealistic character.
- AUTHORIZED_REAL_PERSON: identifiable real person with verified authorization.
- UNVERIFIED: likeness blocked for production.
- RESTRICTED: likeness generation/publication blocked.

## Persistent visual identity
Character profiles preserve face, skin, hair, eyes, body, age, wardrobe and signature features. Generation adapters must preserve stable identity references and provenance.

## Real-person likeness
An identifiable real person requires a recorded authorization/consent reference and verification before likeness generation or publication. WETU never fabricates authorization.

## Continuity
Generation records should preserve identity reference IDs, provider/model metadata when available, timestamp and output hash.

## Product rule
“Identifiable” means the intended fictional character remains recognizable as the same character, or an authorized real person remains recognizable as that person.

## Failure behavior
Missing or invalid authorization blocks real-person likeness and allows a fictional/original workflow instead.
