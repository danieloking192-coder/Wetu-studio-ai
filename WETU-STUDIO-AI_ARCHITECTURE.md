# WETU STUDIO AI — Architecture

## Vision
A modular AI studio for advertising, short films, films, series and social video.

## Core pipeline
1. Project Studio — project identity, type, assets and state.
2. Script Engine — ideas, synopsis, scenes, dialogue and revisions.
3. Character Engine — characters, roles, appearance and continuity.
4. World & Style Bible — locations, visual language, costumes and references.
5. Storyboard Engine — shots, camera, composition and timing.
6. Video Generation Orchestrator — provider adapters, jobs, retries and provenance.
7. Voice & Audio — dialogue, narration, music and sound design.
8. Editing Engine — timeline, cuts, transitions, captions and mastering.
9. Continuity Engine — characters, locations, props, wardrobe and visual consistency.
10. Advertising Studio — briefs, variants, formats, calls-to-action and brand rules.
11. Film & Series Studio — episodes, scenes, sequences and production continuity.
12. Quality & Safety Gate — technical, rights, safety and consistency checks.
13. Export & Publishing — platform-specific renders and metadata.

## Principles
- Modular architecture.
- Provider independence.
- Full project traceability.
- Deterministic metadata and provenance.
- Human validation before irreversible publishing.
- Cost-aware generation and caching.
- Secure secrets and isolated projects.
- Graceful degradation when a provider fails.
